# M1: Mathematical Foundations

Welcome to the bedrock of AI. 

Many students make the mistake of treating the mathematics of AI as a series of disconnected hurdles to jump over before they can "get to the real stuff" (the models). In reality, the mathematics **is** the model. Every layer of a neural network, every optimization step, and every probability distribution is simply an application of the foundations covered here.

## The Connected Mental Model: The Language of Transformation

Instead of seeing these as separate topics, view them as a single system for describing **transformation**:

1. **Functions**: The *act* of transformation. How do we map an input to an output?
2. **Algebra**: The *rules* of transformation. How do we manipulate and solve these mappings?
3. **Exponents & Logarithms**: The *scale* of transformation. How do we handle growth, decay, and the extreme ranges of probability?
4. **Summations**: The *aggregation* of transformation. How do we combine thousands of small changes into a single result?

Once you master these, **Linear Algebra** becomes the study of transformations in high-dimensional space, **Calculus** becomes the study of how those transformations change, and **Probability** becomes the study of transformations under uncertainty.

## Learning Path

This module is designed for mastery. Do not move to the next section until you can not only solve the problems but explain **why** the mechanism works and **how** it manifests in an AI model.

- [ ] **Functions** $\rightarrow$ The core mapping.
- [ ] **Exponents & Logarithms** $\rightarrow$ Handling scale and stability.
- [ ] **Algebra** $\rightarrow$ Symbolic manipulation and normalization.
- [ ] **Summations** $\rightarrow$ Aggregating data and loss.
